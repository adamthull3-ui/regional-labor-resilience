## Primary methodological decisions retained

- September 2018 OMB CBSA delineation, unchanged across years.
- QCEW information cutoff of February 29, 2020 with full-quarter training through June 2019 (but downloaded historical values can include revisions).
- Original per-CBSA Stage 1 winner based on **three 12-month validation origins**; longer choices remain separately flagged sensitivity experiments.
- Distinction between nonnegative positive-deficit C46 and signed 46-month cumulative gap.
- Outer-Census-division geographic holdout and inner-group-only model selection and preprocessing.
- No causal claim that forecast deviation is the identified pandemic effect.

## Authoritative source for QCEW cell handling

BLS QCEW *Questions and Answers*, Question 13: https://www.bls.gov/cew/questions-and-answers.htm . For post-2004 QCEW CSVs the Bureau distinguishes nonexistent cells from suppressed `N` disclosure-coded cells. Nonexistent cells do not require suppression; suppressed values are zero-filled in published files but must remain missing for inference. Do **not** infer that numeric zero in an `N` row is observed zero employment.
